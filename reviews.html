Every review record on one date

The page reads the date's official schedule, then each finished or in-progress game's play-by-play, and lists every reviewDetails record it finds — on the play and on individual pitch events. Review codes are printed as MLB reports them; the reason text comes from MLB's own review-reason registry.

What the counters mean

  • Reviews & challenges — every review record attached to a play (manager challenge, crew-chief, umpire or boundary review).
  • Overturned / Confirmed — reviewDetails.isOverturned with inProgress: false.
  • Under review — reviewDetails.inProgress: true in the last feed read.
  • ABS pitch challenges — review records attached to an individual pitch event, or reporting a pitch-result reason code.
  • Runs on reviewed plays — the play carries official runner movements marked isScoringEvent. It says runs crossed on the play under review; it does not predict what replay will decide.
